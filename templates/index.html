Explainable symptom guide

Apne symptoms samjhiye — har baat ke saath uska evidence

Batayein kya ho raha hai. Main pehle warning signs check karunga, kuch zaroori sawaal poochunga, aur phir NHS, WHO aur MedlinePlus jaise sources ke saath samjhaunga ki kaunse common causes match karte hain — aur kyun.

Kaise kaam karta hai?

Example

naak band hai aur throat mein mucus aa raha hai
Samajh gaya — naak band hona aur gale mein mucus. Yeh allergy ya common cold jaisa kuch ho sakta hai, lekin exact cause abhi confirm nahi hota. Isliye main duration aur bukhar ke baare mein poochunga.
Har conclusion ke saath source: NHS · MedlinePlus · WHO

Yeh tool kya kar sakta hai, kya nahi

Kar sakta hai

  • Common, kam-risk conditions ko compare karna: cold, flu jaisi bimari, naak ki allergy, sinus, gale ki kharash, acidity, kabz, tension headache, migraine, skin irritation
  • Emergency warning signs pehchaan kar turant guidance dena
  • Har baat ka source dikhana taaki aap khud check kar sakein

Nahi kar sakta

  • Diagnosis dena ya doctor ki jagah lena
  • Dawai ya dose batana
  • Serious bimariyon ko rule out karna
